# Agro-Zia Content & Evidence Register — V1

**Purpose:** control public claims and supporting evidence before production publication.

## Status Legend
- `VERIFIED` — supported by an identified source and suitable for publication subject to final QA.
- `EVIDENCE_NEEDED` — content may be useful, but supporting evidence or current-status verification is still required.
- `HISTORICAL` — factual historical information; must not imply a current status.
- `INTERNAL` — retained for internal records and not published publicly.

## Privacy / Non-public Information

The following must not be published on the public website:

- Personal identification numbers and residence/PIN details.
- Private contact or identity information not intended for business publication.
- Confidential contracts or commercially sensitive documents.
- Customer/partner information without publication permission.

## Required Evidence Pack

For each project/product intended for public publication, attach the strongest available source documents, such as:

- official certificate, license or registration;
- signed agreement where relevant;
- manufacturer technical datasheet or label;
- COA/SDS where applicable;
- approved project photographs;
- technical reports, drawings or completion records where publishable.

**Release rule:** no item marked `EVIDENCE_NEEDED` becomes a public factual claim until its evidence and current status are reviewed.
