LEGAL & POLICIES

Responsible digital commerce.

These baseline policies apply to the public AGRO-ZIA website and its B2B inquiry workflow. They are written for the initial launch and do not replace transaction-specific contracts or professional legal advice.

Effective: 10 September 2026 · Version 1.0

Terms of UsePrivacyCommercial Conditions

1. Terms of Use

AGRO-ZIA provides agricultural information, engineering and sourcing support and a B2B platform for business inquiries. By using the website, you agree to use it lawfully and to provide information that you are authorized to share.

Accounts & workspaces

Customer and supplier workspaces are private business areas. Access is controlled by authentication. Users are responsible for keeping access codes and session access secure and for the accuracy of information submitted from their account.

Information & content

Public product, supplier, technical and market information is provided for business evaluation. Specifications, origin, availability, pricing, delivery terms and other commercial details must be confirmed before a transaction.

2. Privacy

When you submit a business inquiry, AGRO-ZIA may process the information you provide, including company, contact, product requirement, destination, timing, message and supporting files.

How information is used

Information is used to review and respond to business requirements, coordinate suppliers or technical resources, maintain request records and operate authenticated customer or supplier workspaces. Operational notifications may be delivered through configured business communication channels.

Storage & retention

Inquiry and transaction records may be stored in the platform database and supporting files may be stored in protected object storage. Records are retained only as reasonably necessary for business operations, security, accounting, dispute handling and applicable legal obligations.

Your request

For questions about personal information, correction or deletion requests, contact AGRO-ZIA through the published contact channels. Identity and authorization may need to be verified before a request is completed.

3. Commercial Conditions

The initial AGRO-ZIA release is quotation-based B2B commerce. Public pages do not constitute a binding offer to sell.

  • Prices are provided by quotation and may depend on quantity, specification, destination, currency, packaging, logistics and timing.
  • Product availability, supplier identity, origin, specifications and delivery conditions are confirmed case by case.
  • A quotation becomes commercially binding only when the applicable parties accept the final terms through an appropriate business document or agreement.
  • Orders, proforma invoices, payment arrangements, shipping and documentation are subject to the terms agreed for the individual transaction.
  • The platform does not represent that it provides escrow, insurance, financing, customs brokerage or another regulated financial or trade service unless a specific supported provider and contractual arrangement are identified.

4. Acceptable Use & Security

Users must not misuse the website, attempt unauthorized access, submit malicious files, impersonate another party, interfere with platform availability or use the service for unlawful activity.

Supporting files

Business inquiries may include supporting documents or images within the limits shown on the inquiry form. Do not upload passwords, payment-card information, identity documents or other unnecessary sensitive material.

External services

Links to services such as WhatsApp or the ZARUS network lead to external services that may have their own terms and privacy policies. AGRO-ZIA does not control those external services.

Initial launch notice

AGRO-ZIA is launching first as a professional B2B inquiry, sourcing, engineering and quotation platform. Online payment capture, automated settlement, escrow and other advanced transaction services are not represented as active unless explicitly supported in the relevant workflow.
